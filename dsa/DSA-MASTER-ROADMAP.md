# DSA Master Roadmap (Python + LeetCode)

> Goal:
>
> Become highly effective at DSA pattern recognition and coding interviews in ~8 weeks.
>
> This roadmap focuses on:
>
> - Pattern Recognition
> - Python Implementation
> - LeetCode Practice
> - Interview Readiness
>
> This is NOT a competitive programming roadmap.
>
> The objective is to become comfortable solving interview-style problems and quickly identifying the correct algorithmic pattern.

---

# Learning Philosophy

The goal is not:

❌ Memorize hundreds of solutions

The goal is:

✅ Recognize patterns quickly

✅ Understand trade-offs

✅ Apply reusable templates

✅ Become confident in interviews
